# Lessons for KOML

The domain requires a distinction between a possible path and a willing introduction. Explicit directed relationships plus organisation affiliations can compute the first. They cannot establish the second. Matching names or assuming reciprocal connections would manufacture evidence.

Validated assumption: on the labelled synthetic 200-record graph, direct and one-intermediary paths can be enumerated deterministically with exact counts and no extra or missing planted paths. Removing a known-contact declaration removes the associated paths, so results depend on inputs rather than canned answers. This validates mechanics only. The demand and real-world accuracy hypotheses remain untested until Henry's two-week trial.

Operating rule: define relationship direction, path depth, identity rules and independent ground truth before implementing a graph tool. Keep raw evidence ids in every path and refuse unsupported outcome claims. Count zero reviewed real paths as unmeasured accuracy, never perfect accuracy.
